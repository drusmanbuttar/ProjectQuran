# Proposed 30 Quranic research categories

These categories follow the founder’s clarification that the original 30 chapters were thematic, not the 30 juz. They are an editable editorial proposal, not a claim to have recovered the original titles or a divinely prescribed division. Verses can belong to several categories. The supplied passage lists are selected starting points, not exhaustive classifications.

## Research method

Read the full passage and recognized tafsir before expanding a category. Preserve the exact Arabic and attributed translations. Record Quran, hadith, history, and researcher interpretation separately. Each historical assertion needs a source; distinguish event date from revelation date. Do not assign calendar dates to creation or the Hereafter. Record disagreements and uncertainty. Treat all new classifications as research drafts until reviewed.
